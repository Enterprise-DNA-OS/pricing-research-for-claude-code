# Why the research desk has no front end

The researcher asks for a study, a segment comparison or a review packet. The agent reads the database through the CLI and prepares the answer. The business keeps the evidence and owns the calculations.

Read-only HTML gives the team a weekly view, a study brief and a decision record without a hosted application. It does not collect survey responses, supply a mobile interviewing experience, provide offline fieldwork, or offer drag-and-drop survey design. A private HTML file also has no built-in access control once shared.

Keep the collection system needed for your studies. Enterprise DNA can scope the collection workflow, specialist methods and screens for your version. The free base is useful after export without those integrations. Never describe it as a replacement for the entire Conjointly platform.

Omni by Enterprise DNA builds and operates the agreed system around the business.
